🔎 What is Sorting?

Sorting is the process of arranging data in a particular order, such as ascending or descending order. It is one of the fundamental operations in computer science.

✨ Why Sorting is Important?

  • Organizes data for easier analysis.
  • Helps improve searching and database operations.
  • Makes ranking, comparing, and finding information easier.

📚 Types of Sorting Techniques

  • Bubble Sort: Compares adjacent elements and swaps them when they are in the wrong order.
  • Selection Sort: Finds the smallest element in the unsorted portion and places it in the correct position.
  • Insertion Sort: Inserts each element into its correct position in the already-sorted portion.

🌍 Real-Life Applications

  • Sorting products by price in online shopping.
  • Ranking students according to their marks.
  • Organizing names, records, and search results.
  • Arranging data for reporting and analysis.
